Privacy Policy

ETA Tickets (support.eta-agency.us)
Expedited Transport Agency (“ETA”, “we”, “us”)
Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ETA collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the ETA Tickets help-desk service at https://support.eta-agency.us, including optional SMS (text message) notifications.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to ETA employees and authorized users who sign in to ETA Tickets and, where enabled, opt in to receive SMS updates about their IT support tickets.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, work email address, and authentication details provided through our identity provider.
  • Ticket content — descriptions, messages, attachments, device or account context, and related support metadata you submit or that agents add while resolving tickets.
  • Contact preferences — notification settings, including whether you opt in to SMS.
  • Phone number — mobile number you provide for SMS, plus verification status if we confirm ownership of that number.
  • Technical logs — approximate usage and delivery logs needed to operate the service (for example sign-in events, SMS send status, and error diagnostics).

3. How we use information

  • Provide and secure the ETA Tickets service.
  • Route, assign, and resolve support requests.
  • Send email and, if you opt in, SMS notifications about ticket activity (for example new tickets for IT agents, public replies, assignment/claim, and resolved/closed status).
  • Verify your phone number when you enable SMS.
  • Maintain service reliability, security, and audit trails.

We do not use SMS for marketing, advertising, or promotional campaigns.

4. SMS / text messaging

SMS is optional. Message frequency varies based on ticket activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of SMS at any time by turning off SMS notifications in ETA Tickets settings, or by replying STOP to an ETA Tickets text. For help, reply HELP or email it@eta-agency.us.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We use a messaging provider (currently Twilio) to deliver SMS. That provider processes phone numbers and message content solely to send notifications on our behalf.

5. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run ETA Tickets:

  • With ETA IT staff and authorized agents working your tickets.
  • With service providers that host or deliver the product (for example cloud hosting, email, and SMS delivery), under agreements that limit use to providing those services.
  • When required by law, or to protect the security and integrity of ETA systems and users.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share phone numbers with third parties for their own marketing.

6. Retention

We retain ticket records, account data, and related logs for as long as needed for support, security, and business operations, then delete or de-identify them according to ETA’s internal retention practices. If you disable SMS or remove your phone number, we stop using it for new SMS notifications; limited delivery logs may remain for a period for troubleshooting and compliance.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to an internal enterprise help-desk system, including authenticated access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices

  • Update notification preferences and phone number in ETA Tickets settings.
  • Opt out of SMS as described above.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting it@eta-agency.us, subject to ETA’s employment and records obligations.

9. Children’s privacy

ETA Tickets is an internal workplace tool and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use of ETA Tickets after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or SMS notifications:
Email: it@eta-agency.us
Service: https://support.eta-agency.us
